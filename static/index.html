Honest notes. Fossil records show what has been found near here, not everything that lived here.

Whether a place was sea, land or swamp is inferred from the rock type, so it is only ever "probably".

Animal records show where an animal was observed, not necessarily where it lives.

The narration is written by an open-weight AI model that is only given the facts shown on this page. Check the cards below it.

The scribe, the stone tablets and the layers are original decoration. They are not a picture of any real culture or period, and the layers are for orientation, not to scale.
